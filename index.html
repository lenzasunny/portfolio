About Me

I am a 3D Artist and Virtual Human/XR Researcher with seven years of university-based research experience in digital avatars, immersive VR/XR, character animation, motion capture, and real-time interactive systems.

At NxR Lab, Ajou University, I contributed to virtual-human research, VR games, interactive applications, character creation, animation, motion-capture workflows, and Unity-based content development. My research interests include expressive digital humans, speech-driven avatars, virtual-human interaction, and immersive HCI.

I also have experience in 3D graphics, motion graphics, video production, and multimedia design, using tools such as Blender, Maya, Character Creator, iClone, Unity, Unreal Engine, Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, and Premiere Pro.
