About Me

I am a 3D Artist and Virtual Human/XR Researcher with seven years of university-based research experience in digital avatars, immersive VR/XR, character animation, motion capture, and real-time interactive systems.

At NxR Lab, Ajou University , I contributed to virtual-human research, VR games, interactive applications, character creation, animation, motion-capture workflows, and Unity-based content development. My research interests include immersive interaction, virtual humans, role-play and perspective-taking experiences, experiential learning, speech-driven avatars, and human-computer interaction in XR environments. I am particularly interested in how immersive technologies can be used to help people learn, experience different perspectives, and interact with virtual characters in meaningful ways.

I also have experience in 3D graphics, motion graphics, video production, and multimedia design, using tools such as Blender, Maya, Character Creator, iClone, Unity, Unreal Engine, Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, and Premiere Pro.
